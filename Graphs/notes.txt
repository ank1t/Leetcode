Nodes are also called as vertex or vertices

Edges connect 2 nodes

Types:
- Undirected graphs

- Directed graphs

Trees are a type of graph because they have nodes, edges and are undirected.

Cycles in a graph - Starting from a node and moving in 1 direction, if we can reach the same node it means that there is a cycle in the graph.

PATH: can contain a lot of nodes and each of them are reachable. A node cannot appear twice in a path.

Degress in aN UNDIRECTED graph: Number of edges that that end or emanate from a node.

DEGRESS IN A DIRECTED GRAPH: INDEGREE AND OUTDEGREE.

EDGE WEIGHTS:
*** TOTAL DEGREE OF A GRAPH IS TWICE THE NUMBER OF EDGES i.e n(DEGREES) == 2 * E ***

Topological sorting
Only applies to DAG(Directed acyclic graph). Its a linear ordering of vertices such that if there is and edge between
U and V, U appears before V in ordering.

There can be multiple valid ordering.
